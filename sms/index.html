SMS Program

Operated by Mahesh (sole proprietor) · Filter Coffee Way · Updated 30 August 2026

This is a personal errand-assistant text messaging program. It sends transactional order-status messages to a single recipient: the account owner. It is not open to the public and does not accept sign-ups from other people.

Opt-in disclosure

By texting an order keyword such as COFFEE to the program number, you agree to receive transactional text messages from Mahesh (Filter Coffee Way) about that order.

Message frequency varies and is determined by you — typically fewer than 10 messages per day, and often none. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to cancel at any time.

See our Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

How to opt in

  1. Text a keyword to the program number. Sending an order keyword (for example COFFEE), or START, YES or UNSTOP, opts you in.
  2. You receive an immediate confirmation naming the program, confirming enrollment, and giving help and opt-out instructions.

The confirmation message is:

Mahesh: You are now opted in to receive messages. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

What the messages look like

You text:

coffee

You receive an order summary and a spending limit, and are asked to confirm. Nothing is ordered until you reply:

1 drip coffee (medium) for pickup, under Mahesh · cap $5.75 — reply Y to dial

You reply:

Y

The order is placed by phone, and you receive the result:

☕ drip coffee confirmed · $3.25 · ready 4:12 · #47 — reply N if wrong

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message to stop all messages. You will receive one confirmation and nothing further. Reply START, YES or UNSTOP to resume.

Help

Reply HELP to any message, or email filtercoffeeway@gmail.com.
