URL Shortener

A read-heavy redirect at planet scale, designed around a single elegant constraint: never make the user wait.

Published 25 May 2026 · Medium read-heavy caching base62 NoSQL

Shortening a URL is easy. Doing it at a hundred thousand requests per second, with five-nines availability and sub-ten-millisecond redirects, is where the trade-offs begin. This is a walk through the decisions: counter ranges versus hashing, Cassandra versus Postgres, where the cache earns its keep, and which mistakes look fine until traffic doubles.
