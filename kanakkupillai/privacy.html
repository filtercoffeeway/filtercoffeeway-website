Privacy Policy

KanakkuPillai

KanakkuPillai is a private bill-reminder tool used by one household. It reads bank, card and bill emails that are forwarded to one dedicated Gmail account, and sends reminders and low-balance alerts to a private Telegram group. This page explains what data it reads, where that data goes, and why.

Effective: September 23, 2026  ·  Applies to: the "KanakkuPillai" Google OAuth app

Summary

KanakkuPillai is not a public product. Its only users are the members of the household that runs it, and the only Google account it connects to is a dedicated Gmail account that household owns. It has read-only access to that account's email. It cannot send, change or delete email. It never logs in to a bank and cannot move money. The data it reads is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared except as described below.

What it reads

KanakkuPillai reads only the messages under one label of the dedicated Gmail account. These are emails the household forwards to it:

  • Bank balance alerts: the account balance and the last four digits of the account number.
  • Credit card statement and payment emails: statement balance, minimum payment, due date, amount paid, and the last four digits of the card.
  • Utility and other bill emails: amount due and due date.

It also reads photos of paper bills and short notes that household members send to it in their private Telegram group.

Google data access

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly
Lets KanakkuPillai list and read messages in the dedicated account, so it can find amounts, balances and due dates in bank, card and bill emails. Read-only: it cannot send, modify or delete anything.

KanakkuPillai's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to provide the bill reminders and balance alerts described here. It is not transferred to anyone else except as needed to provide that feature (see Third parties), is not used for advertising, and is not read by any person other than the household members themselves.

Where data is stored

Everything KanakkuPillai extracts, along with the text of the emails it processed, is stored in a database on a small home server owned by the household. Nightly backups go to a second server in the same home. Nothing is stored on any Filter Coffee Way server or in any cloud database. The Gmail access token is stored on the same home server.

Third parties

  • Anthropic (Claude API). The text of each email, and any photo or note sent in Telegram, is sent to Anthropic's API only to extract amounts and dates. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms and does not use API data to train its models.
  • Telegram. Reminders and alerts, which include bill names, amounts, due dates and account balances, are delivered to the household's private Telegram group through the Telegram Bot API.
  • Google. Emails are read from Gmail through the Gmail API.

There are no analytics, advertising or tracking services, and no data is sold or shared with anyone else.

Your control over your data

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the dedicated account's Google Account → Third-party connections page. That stops all further reading of email. The stored data can be deleted by deleting the database and backups on the home server.

Children's privacy

KanakkuPillai is not directed at children. It is used only by the adults of one household.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
